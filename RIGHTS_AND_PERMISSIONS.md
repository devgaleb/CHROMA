CHROMA is the name of this application. Copyright in original code, visual assets,
and documentation belongs to their respective creators. The project maintainer
should replace generic copyright statements with their verified legal name or
organisation before public distribution.

## Permissions for CHROMA-owned work

Unless an individual file explicitly provides a different licence, the project
owner reserves the rights to reproduce, distribute, modify and commercially
exploit their original work. Permission to view or download the source does not
by itself authorise reposting, selling, rebranding or publishing a modified copy.

Requests for permission should identify the files, intended use, whether changes
will be published, and the intended distribution channel. Permission is effective
only when granted in writing by the rights holder.

## Third-party components

The project owner's restrictions cannot override third-party open-source
licences. Libraries, pretrained model weights, and reused code are governed by
their respective licences. Review the dependency and model licence terms before
public release. Preserve required notices and source disclosures.

## Authorship and enforcement

Keep dated source commits, original artwork, release archives and written
contributor agreements. When reporting a suspected unauthorised repost, provide
specific original works and matching copied material; avoid unsupported claims.

No text file, watermark or licence can physically prevent copying. This document
clarifies intended permissions and is not legal advice.
