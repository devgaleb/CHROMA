# CHROMA reuse and redistribution policy

CHROMA's original code and brand assets are **all rights reserved** unless an
individual file states otherwise. Public visibility of a repository is not
permission to copy, sell, repackage, remove credits, or repost the project.

## What is allowed?

No general reuse licence is offered for CHROMA-owned material. Contact the
project rights holder to request permission. Dependencies retain their own
licences, which may grant additional rights to those dependencies only.

## Reporting unauthorised reposts

Preserve the original commit history, timestamps, release archives and evidence
of your authorship. Record the allegedly copied URL, affected original files,
and dates. Use the host's copyright-reporting process where appropriate.
Avoid publishing personal details or making unsupported accusations.

## Limitations

A licence notice cannot technically stop copying, and copyright protection
applies only to material you actually own or are authorised to license.
